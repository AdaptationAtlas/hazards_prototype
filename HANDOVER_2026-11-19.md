## 1. Next: send U0–U4 to CGlabs

CGlabs' V0–V2 reply (`ded544a`, 2026-11-18) passed every gate: livestock rows are now weighted in TLU, and the
area and crop-production rows are unchanged. It also flagged `p_mk` values no 43-year series can produce (3.9e-42,
an exact 0). **That was a real bug in `mk_sen()`** (`R/obs/s7_trends_anomalies.R`). The Hamed-Rao autocorrelation
factor was floored at 1e-6, so negative lag correlations could shrink the Mann-Kendall variance a million-fold.
It is now floored at 1, so the correction can only make a trend look less significant, never more. Selftest 23.
It changes `p_mk` and `sig_fdr` in the trend rasters and admin trend tables, and nothing else. The block gates
exactly that: slope, normals and series identical; p never smaller; no significance gained.
