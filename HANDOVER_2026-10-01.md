## Conventions this thread earned

- **Judge a re-baked product against the input it was built from, on the same grid.** Not against the
  live object, and not against an "expected move" table from another grid: R/3 is a linear multiply and
  `freq_any + freq_none = 1`, so the product's admin0 total *is* its exposure total. The live-vs-new
  drift is a report, the product-vs-input ratio is the gate. Small countries make any cross-grid
  comparison lie (RWA/GAB coffee "expected 87×").
- **A gate's reference must be independent of the table it judges.** With oil palm contaminated in 17
  of 21 countries, the crop median implied price was itself contaminated and flagged the one sane
  country. Use the world price.
- **National-total reconciliation cannot see a per-crop or per-country misallocation.** Sudan, millet
  and oil palm all passed the FAOSTAT total gates. Cross-basis per pair is the check that sees them.
- **Park, never `rm`; park probe outputs too; "left behind = 0" before launch.** Skip-if-exists turns an
  incomplete park into a half-stale product.
- **Background anything that uploads.** The node's interactive shell kills foreground commands after
  about two minutes; a publish was SIGTERM'd mid-run (the publisher is idempotent and recovered).
- **Check `origin/develop` before re-sending a prompt.** cglabs rightly refused a stale re-send of an
  already-run block; the author check on the watch applies to your own re-sends too.
- **The repository is public.** No internal addresses, hostnames or mount identifiers in tracked files.
