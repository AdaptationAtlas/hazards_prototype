Seasonal Climate Hazard Time Series by Administrative Unit, Africa (NEX-GDDP-CMIP6 18-model ensemble)

Description

Year-by-year values of nine agro-climatic hazard indices (rainfall, temperature, heat-stress days, dry days, soil water stress, waterlogging, and human and livestock heat stress) for every country and first-level administrative unit in Africa, for each rolling three-month season and for the whole year. Each row summarises 18 bias-corrected, downscaled climate models from NASA's NEX-GDDP-CMIP6: the ensemble mean, the spread between models (standard deviation and 17th-83rd percentile range, the IPCC "likely" range), and the same statistics for the anomaly from a historical baseline average. Historical years (1981-2014) come from the models' historical runs; future years cover 2021-2100 under four emissions scenarios. Use it to chart how a hazard is projected to evolve in a region and season, and how much the models disagree.
